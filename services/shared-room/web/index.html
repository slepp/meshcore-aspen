Radio delivery

This message is saved in the room. Radio ACKs confirm receipt by radio clients, not that a person read it. Counts include waiting room-alias/client sessions and retained delivery attempts; a radio joined to two aliases can count twice.

Waiting includes sessions blocked behind earlier history, not positions in a transmit queue. Paused overlaps the delivery counts: its frontend is disconnected or no longer authorized for that delivery. Radio sessions persist when a radio goes away; these counts are not online participants.

Transmitted means the frontend confirmed transmission, not reception. Exhausted deliveries still accept a late ACK. Rejoin from the companion app to resume its history; do not resend the post.
